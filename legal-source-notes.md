## Legal wording verification

The official Cyprus Data Protection Commissioner search results identify Law 125(I)/2018 and the official law PDF. The official result snippets state that Article 8 of Law 125(I)/2018 concerns processing of children's personal data below age 14 and parental consent/authorisation for information-society services. The direct PDF URL redirected in the browser to the current official Commissioner portal, and direct text extraction of the PDF was unavailable. The website wording should therefore be cautious: it can state that the site observes the applicable requirements of Law 125(I)/2018 and that, for information-society services relying on consent, parental authorisation is required below age 14. This is not a claim that all processing is automatically lawful at age 14.
